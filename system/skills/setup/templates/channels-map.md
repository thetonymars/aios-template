# Channels

Where and how this business reaches people.

| File | What | A new fact goes to |
|---|---|---|
| `channels.md` | the list of channels: the handle, whether statistics are collected | a new channel, a renamed one, a channel switched off |
| `links.md` | the registry of short links (slug ↔ material ↔ offer) and the rule for UTM names | a new short link |
| `site.md` | the rule for the page addresses of the business's site, when it has one | a change of that rule |

## What does not live here

- Channel statistics — `../data/` (they are collected by a skill, never by hand).
- Offers and their links — `../offers/`.

## Rules

- A channel added or renamed in `channels.md`: `channels.md` says where else the same name is written. Change those places in the same step.
